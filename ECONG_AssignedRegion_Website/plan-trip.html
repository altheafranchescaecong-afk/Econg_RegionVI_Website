⚓︎ Essential Travel Tips

  • Best Time to Visit: November to April during the dry season for optimal beach and island-hopping weather.
  • Currency & Cash: Carry Philippine Pesos (PHP) in cash, especially when visiting eco-parks, smaller islands, and local markets.
  • Getting Around: Tricycles, jeepneys, and taxis are widely available for local transport, while ferries connect the islands.
  • Respect Local Customs: Dress appropriately when visiting historical churches and dispose of waste properly to protect marine life.

⚓︎ Basic Packing Checklist

  • Lightweight, breathable cotton clothing and swimwear
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, and a wide-brimmed hat
  • Waterproof dry bag for boat tours and water activities
  • Sturdy walking shoes for heritage walks and trail hikes
  • Universal power adapter and power bank
  • Personal medications and insect repellent
