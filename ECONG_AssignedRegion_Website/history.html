༄ Our History & Culture ༄

Rich Culture & Traditions

Western Visayas is deeply rooted in vibrant traditions, historical milestones, and world-renowned festivals like the Ati-Atihan in Aklan and the Dinagyang Festival in Iloilo.

These annual celebrations reflect the region's strong indigenous heritage, Spanish colonial influences, and the joyful, resilient spirit of the Visayan people.

A Tapestry of Heritage

Western Visayas boasts a rich historical narrative deeply interwoven with ancient legends, colonial transformations, and agricultural prosperity. According to oral history and local lore recorded in the Maragtas, ten Malay datus fleeing Borneo arrived on the shores of Panay Island during the 13th century, peacefully trading a golden salakot and necklace with the native Aeta people to settle and cultivate the fertile lands.

During the Spanish colonial era, Region VI emerged as a prominent spiritual and economic hub in the Philippines. Spanish friars established majestic stone churches across Panay and Negros, including the UNESCO World Heritage Miagao Church in Iloilo. The 19th century brought an agricultural boom, turning Iloilo into a major port for textile trading and Negros Occidental into the grand "Sugarbowl of the Philippines," spawning wealthy estates and elegant ancestral architecture.

Today, the enduring resilience and warmth of the Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a, and Aklanon people continue to preserve these centuries-old traditions. Through world-renowned street festivals, intricate craftsmanship, and timeless culinary recipes, Western Visayas honors its past while warmly welcoming the world.

Cultural Highlights

𓇼 Vibrant Festivals

  • Ati-Atihan Festival (Kalibo, Aklan): Known as the "Mother of All Philippine Festivals," featuring energetic drum rhythms, street dancing, and soot-painted participants honoring Santo Niño.
  • Dinagyang Festival (Iloilo City): A world-famous religious and cultural festival celebrated with dramatic choreography, vibrant costumes, and thunderous drumming.
  • MassKara Festival (Bacolod City, Negros Occidental): The "City of Smiles" comes alive every October with colorful, smiling masks, street dancing, and lively music.
  • Manggahan Festival (Guimaras): A month-long celebration showcasing the province's world-famous sweet mangoes and local cultural heritage.
  • Binirayan Festival (Antique): Commemorates the legendary arrival of the ten Bornean datus on Panay Island with theatrical re-enactments.

𓇼 Traditional Crafts & Iconic Foods

  • Hablon & Piña Weaving: Traditional handloom weaving of delicate pineapple and abaca fibers into exquisite fabrics in Iloilo and Aklan.
  • Bariw & Pandan Handcrafts: Intricate handwoven mats, bags, and hats made from native plant leaves in Antique.
  • La Paz Batchoy & Pancit Molo (Iloilo): Classic, comforting noodle dishes enriched with pork cracklings, garlic, and savory bone broth.
  • Chicken Inasal & Piaya (Bacolod): Annatto-marinated charcoal-grilled chicken paired with sweet, muscovado-filled flatbreads.
  • Fresh Capiz Shell Products & Seafood: Translucent windowpane oyster shells crafted into home decor, alongside world-class fresh seafood.
