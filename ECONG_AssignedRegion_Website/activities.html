  • Boracay Water Sports (Aklan): Parasailing, kitesurfing, and paddleboarding at Bulabog and White Beach.
  • Tibiao Kawa Hot Bath & River Tubing (Antique): Relax in giant herbal cauldron hot baths and go whitewater river tubing.
  • Mararison Island Trekking (Antique): Hike rolling green hills and walk the shifting sandbar off Culasi.
  • Islas de Gigantes Lagoon Exploring (Iloilo): Island hop around Tangke Saltwater Lagoon and limestone cliffs.
  • Sipalay Beach & Diving (Negros Occidental): Explore sunken shipwrecks and vibrant coral reefs.
